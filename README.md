## 3) Problem Statement
Build an Inclination Score, a message-by-message estimate of a
customer's likelihood to purchase.

Sales chats (e.g., a customer asking about a credit card) contain clues about
whether the customer will buy.
Identify the turning points where interest rises or drops, and what triggered
them.
Why it matters: helps sales teams prioritize promising leads and improve chat
scripts.
